Session 7 of 10 · Representation learning and self-supervision

[1 click] The pipeline has two stages. First, an encoder f is trained on unlabelled data with a self-supervised objective. This is the expensive step, and it is done once. The encoder maps a raw input x, an image, a sequence, a molecule, to a vector z of a few hundred numbers: the representation. Second, for each task, a small head is trained on top of z with the few labels available: a linear layer, or a small network. The same encoder serves many tasks: graft-loss prediction, rejection, phenotyping, retrieval of similar patients. Everything in this session is about the first stage.
