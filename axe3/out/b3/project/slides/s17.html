Session 7 of 10 · Representation learning and self-supervision

[1 click] This is the slide I would like you to remember. A view is a statement: these two inputs should have the same representation. In other words, an invariance. If the invariance is wrong, the encoder is trained to delete signal. Colour jitter is harmless for photographs of dogs; in histology, stain colour is diagnostic. For molecules, a small edit can change activity by orders of magnitude: so-called activity cliffs. For clinical sequences, shifting timestamps or rescaling doses would make the encoder blind to exactly what pharmacology is about. Masking and predictive objectives are often safer than augmentation here, because they assume less.
