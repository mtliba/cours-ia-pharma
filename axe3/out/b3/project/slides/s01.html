Representation learning and self-supervision

Learning from unlabelled data: contrastive, non-contrastive, masked and predictive objectives.

Marouane Tliba

Session seven. This is the conceptual centre of the course. Foundation models, in session nine, are large encoders trained with the objectives we study today. The question is simple to state: how do we learn a useful description of data without labels? I will insist on what each objective assumes, because those assumptions are where transfer to clinical and pharmacological data succeeds or fails.
