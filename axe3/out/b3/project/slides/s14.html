Session 7 of 10 · Representation learning and self-supervision

[1 click] The second large family: masked modelling. Hide a random set M of positions; the encoder sees only the visible part; a light decoder predicts the hidden values; the loss is the reconstruction error on the masked positions. For categorical tokens, replace the squared error by a cross-entropy: that is BERT. The decoder is thrown away afterwards; we keep the encoder. One parameter matters: the mask ratio. Images are highly redundant, so the masked autoencoder hides seventy-five percent of the patches, otherwise the task is solved by local interpolation. Text is dense, so BERT hides fifteen percent. For a new modality, ask how redundant it is.
