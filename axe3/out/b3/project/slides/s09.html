The InfoNCE loss

A classification problem: find the positive among all candidates.

Embeddings of the two views of the same example: the positive pair.

Cosine similarity: the dot product of the two vectors after normalizing them to length 1.

Sum over all other embeddings in the batch: the positive and 2N − 2 negatives, for a batch of N examples.

The temperature: a small positive number that sharpens the distribution.

Cross-entropy over candidates: the label is “which one is the other view of me?”

Session 7 of 10 · Representation learning and self-supervision

[1 click] Here is the loss. Read it as a classification problem. For the anchor z i, there are two N minus one candidates in the batch: one positive, z j, and the rest negatives. We compute the cosine similarity of the anchor with each candidate, divide by a temperature tau, and apply a softmax. The loss is the negative log-probability assigned to the positive. It is the cross-entropy of session five, where the classes are the candidates. Minimizing it maximizes a lower bound on the mutual information between the two views, and that bound cannot exceed the logarithm of the number of candidates: one reason large batches help.
