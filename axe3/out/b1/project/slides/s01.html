Part II · Advanced track · Session 5 of 10

Optimization and generalization in deep networks

How deep networks are trained, why training fails, and why very large models still generalize.

Marouane Tliba

Welcome to the second part of the course. Part one, in French, gave the vocabulary: data, model, loss, generalization. Part two is for people who will build and publish models: master's students in research tracks, PhD students, and researchers. Each session follows the same pattern: the mathematical object, every symbol explained, a figure, a few lines of code, an exercise. We start with the engine room: optimization. Everything later in the course, transformers, self-supervision, diffusion, is trained with the tools of this session.
