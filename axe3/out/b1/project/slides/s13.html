Three ideas that made depth trainable

Without them, signals and gradients vanish or explode across layers.

Initialization

Draw weights with variance about 2 / fan-in (He) or 1 / fan-in (Xavier), so that activations and gradients keep the same scale from layer to layer.

Normalization

BatchNorm standardizes each feature over the batch; LayerNorm standardizes each example over its features. Both keep activations in a stable range. LayerNorm does not depend on the batch: it is the choice for transformers and small batches.

Residual connections

Each block computes a correction that is added to its input. The identity path carries the gradient straight to early layers.

Session 5 of 10 · Optimization and generalization in deep networks

Thirty layers of plain fully connected units do not train: the signal either dies or explodes on the way. Three ideas fixed this. Initialization: choose the variance of the initial weights so that activations and gradients keep the same scale from layer to layer. For ReLU, that is variance two over fan-in, the He initialization. Normalization: standardize activations inside the network. BatchNorm does it across the batch, LayerNorm across the features of each example. LayerNorm does not depend on batch statistics, which is why transformers use it, and why it is safer with small clinical batches. Residual connections are the third idea, on the next slide.
