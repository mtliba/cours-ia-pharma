Session 6 of 10 · Attention and transformers

[1 click] Two rules of thumb. Parameters: each block has four d-squared weights in attention, for the query, key, value and output projections, and eight d-squared in an MLP with the usual four-fold expansion. Twelve d-squared per block. With d seven hundred sixty-eight and twelve blocks, that is eighty-five million, and about a hundred and ten million with the embedding table: BERT-base. Cost: attention compares every token with every other, so time and memory grow with the square of the sequence length. Clinical sequences are short; a whole-slide image is millions of patches and needs a hierarchy.
