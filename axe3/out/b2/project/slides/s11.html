[1 click] A detail that matters a great deal for clinical data. Shuffle the rows of X and the attention output is shuffled the same way: attention has no notion of order. We must inject it. The original recipe adds to each token a vector of sines and cosines of its position, at geometrically spaced frequencies; the figure shows three of them. Low frequencies encode coarse position, high frequencies fine position. For text, t is the rank of the word. For a patient record, use the actual time: days since transplant, not visit number. Two visits three days apart and two visits three months apart are not the same thing, and visit rank cannot tell them apart.
