Learning objectives

By the end of this session you will be able to:

Turn text, molecules, images and clinical events into sequences of tokens.

Write scaled dot-product attention and explain every term.

Describe a transformer block and the three model families built from it.

Estimate the parameter count and the cost of a transformer.

Session 6 of 10 · Attention and transformers

Four objectives. Turn any modality into a sequence of tokens. Write the attention equation and explain every term in it. Describe a transformer block, and the three families of models built from it: encoder-only, decoder-only, encoder-decoder. And estimate the size and the cost of a transformer before you train it.
