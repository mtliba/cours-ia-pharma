Session 6 of 10 · Attention and transformers

Here is what the softmax output looks like. Rows are the tokens being updated, columns are the tokens they look at. Each row sums to one. Look at the last row, the level we want to predict. It puts 0.41 of its weight on the last dose, 0.30 on the previous level, and little on the transplant date. The grey cells are masked: an event may not look at the future. A caution that I will repeat: these weights are illustrative, I set the scores by hand. And even in a trained model, an attention map is not an explanation of the prediction.
