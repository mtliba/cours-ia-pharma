When something was measured is information. Resampling to a regular grid throws it away.

Session 10 of 10 · Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

[1 click] This is a schematic of one year for one patient. Three rows: tacrolimus levels, creatinine, dose changes. In the first weeks after transplantation, measurements are dense. Then they thin out. In pink, eleven weeks with no visit at all. In light blue, three creatinine tests within one week, and two dose changes: something happened. If you resample this onto a regular monthly grid and fill the gaps with the last value, you destroy both signals: the gap and the burst. A model for these data must take the timestamps as they are.
