Session 10 of 10 · Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

[1 click] The last diagram. A model output is an input to a decision. The model produces a prediction with its uncertainty: a calibrated risk, or a conformal interval from session nine. If the uncertainty is too large, or the patient is unlike the training population, the system abstains and says why. Otherwise the suggestion is shown together with its interval, never as a bare number. The clinician decides and may overrule. The decision and the outcome are logged, which feeds both the evaluation and the next version of the model. The design question is not only accuracy. It is whether the clinician can tell when to trust the output, and automation bias is the known risk.
