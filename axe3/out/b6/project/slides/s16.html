Three routes from observational data to an interventional claim.

State the assumptions. No unmeasured confounding; every patient could have received either option; well-defined interventions.

Session 10 of 10 · Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

So when may a prediction support a decision? Three routes. First, state the assumptions under which an effect can be estimated from observational data: no unmeasured confounding, positivity, meaning every kind of patient could have received either option, and well-defined interventions. Second, emulate a target trial: write the protocol of the randomized trial you would like to run, eligibility, treatment strategies, time zero, outcome, and analyse the observational data to mimic it. Third, use the mechanism. A pharmacokinetic model genuinely answers a what-if question about exposure, because it encodes how dose produces concentration. The link from exposure to clinical outcome is the open part. And in every case: prospective evaluation.
