Session 10 of 10 · Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

[1 click] An example of the third route, from the Limoges pharmacology unit. The difficulty with machine learning for exposure estimation is the lack of large databases of full concentration profiles. The authors took a published population pharmacokinetic model of tacrolimus and simulated nine thousand rich profiles. They trained a gradient-boosted model to estimate the area under the curve from two or three concentrations and a few covariates. Then they evaluated it on four independent datasets of real transplant patients with rich sampling. Relative bias was under five percent and relative root-mean-square error under ten percent, comparable to Bayesian estimation. Mechanistic knowledge supplied the training data; real patients supplied the verdict.
