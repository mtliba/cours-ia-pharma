Session 10 of 10 · Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

Five takeaways for this session, and in a sense for the course. Encode events as code, value and real time. Use the mechanism: learn its parameters, learn a correction to it, or learn from its simulations. Fuse modalities at an intermediate level, and train with modalities dropped. A prediction answers what will happen; a decision needs what if: say which one you claim. And evaluate up to usefulness, with a human who can overrule.
