Tokens: events (code, value, real time), with dose and time since last dose. Split: by patient, and by period or centre for the test set.

Baseline: population pharmacokinetic model with Bayesian estimation; also “last value carried forward”. Metric: relative error in ng/mL terms, with calibration of the intervals.

Pitfall: doses in the data were chosen from earlier levels. The model learned to predict under that policy, not what a different dose would do.
