Learning objectives

By the end of this session you will be able to:

Represent irregular, multimodal clinical sequences for a transformer.

Distinguish a prediction from the effect of an intervention.

Session 10 of 10 · Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

Four objectives. Represent irregular, multimodal clinical sequences in a form a transformer can read. Combine mechanistic pharmacokinetic models with learned components, in three different ways. Distinguish a prediction from the effect of an intervention, and say what each requires. And evaluate a model for clinical usefulness, not only for accuracy.
