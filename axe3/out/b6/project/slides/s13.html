Session 10 of 10 · Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

[1 click] Intermediate fusion as a diagram. On the left, three sources: the sequence of doses, levels and laboratory values; the reports; the biopsy images. Each has its own encoder, often a pre-trained foundation model adapted as in session nine. Their outputs are sequences of tokens. A fusion transformer lets the tokens of all modalities attend to each other. On the right, several heads read the fused representation: the risk of graft loss, the next drug level, a phenotype. Training several heads together is multi-task learning, and it usually helps when labels for each task are scarce.
