Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

Irregular time, several modalities, mechanistic knowledge, and the step from a prediction to a decision about a dose.

Marouane Tliba

The last session. We bring the whole course to bear on the data of clinical pharmacology. These data have four properties that standard benchmarks do not have. They are irregular in time. They are multimodal. They obey known mechanisms, which we should use. And they were generated by clinicians making decisions, which makes the step from prediction to decision delicate. This session is closest to open research questions; I will say so where that is the case.
