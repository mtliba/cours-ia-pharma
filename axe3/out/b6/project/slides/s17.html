Session 10 of 10 · Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

[1 click] Evaluation has levels, and most publications stop at the first. Discrimination: does the model rank patients correctly? Calibration: do predicted risks match observed frequencies, overall and in subgroups? Transportability: does it work in another centre, in a later period? Clinical usefulness: if clinicians act on it at a given risk threshold, does that do more good than harm? And impact: do patients actually do better when clinicians have the tool? That last level needs a prospective study. Reporting should follow the TRIPOD plus AI statement, and the course on responsible AI treats the first three levels in detail.
