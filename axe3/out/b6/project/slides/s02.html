Session 10 of 10 · Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

One patient. A kidney-transplant recipient followed for three years. Tacrolimus trough levels, measured at irregular visits. Creatinine. Doses that were changed in response to those levels. A genotype. Biopsy reports in free text. And long periods with no measurement at all. Two questions. How do we build a model of such a trajectory? And the harder one: suppose the model predicts well. When may that prediction support a decision about the dose? The second question is not a machine-learning question only; it is a causal one.
