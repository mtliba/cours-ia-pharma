Session 10 of 10 · Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

A patient is described by numbers, codes, text and images. Where should the modalities meet? Early fusion concatenates inputs: simple, but it needs every modality to be present and aligned. Late fusion trains one model per modality and combines predictions: robust when a modality is missing, blind to interactions. Intermediate fusion is the current default: one encoder per modality, then tokens from all modalities enter a shared transformer, or attend to each other through cross-attention. And contrastive alignment, from session seven, pre-trains the encoders on unlabelled pairs. In every case, train with modalities randomly dropped: in the clinic, something is always missing.
