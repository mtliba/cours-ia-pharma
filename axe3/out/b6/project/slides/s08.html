Continuous-time hidden states

Let the state evolve between observations.

A hidden state defined at every instant, not only at visits.

A neural network that gives the rate of change of the state.

A numerical solver carries the state from one observation time to the next, whatever the gap.

Neural ODEs (Chen et al., 2018); latent ODEs for irregular series (Rubanova et al., 2019).

A compartmental model is a differential equation with a hand-written right-hand side.

[1 click] A second way to handle irregular time: make the model continuous. Instead of a hidden state updated at each observation, define a state h of t that exists at every instant, and let a neural network give its derivative. To go from one observation to the next, integrate the differential equation with a numerical solver, over whatever gap separates them. This is a neural ordinary differential equation. Gradients flow through the solver. A pharmacologist will recognize the form: a compartmental model is exactly this, with a hand-written right-hand side. That observation leads to the next part.
