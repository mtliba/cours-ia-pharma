Session 10 of 10 · Multimodal and longitudinal health data: from models to decisions

Here is what pharmacology already knows. A one-compartment model with first-order absorption. Two equations. The amount of drug in the gut decreases at rate k a. The concentration in blood increases with absorption and decreases with elimination, clearance over volume. Three parameters, each with a physiological meaning, each different from patient to patient: that variability is what population pharmacokinetics models. The figure is a simulation with illustrative parameters: three milligrams every twelve hours, a trough near 10 and a peak near 19 nanograms per millilitre. In red, the troughs: the only points a clinician usually sees. A network that ignores this structure must rediscover it from sparse points.
