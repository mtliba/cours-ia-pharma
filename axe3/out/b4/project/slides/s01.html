Part II · Advanced track · Session 8 of 10

Deep generative models

Autoregressive models, variational autoencoders, diffusion and flow matching, with their uses in drug discovery and patient modelling.

Marouane Tliba

Session eight: generative models. So far our models mapped an input to a prediction. Today the model produces new data: a sentence, a molecule, a protein backbone, a patient trajectory. I will cover five families, with their objective functions written out. Two of them carry most of current practice: autoregressive models for anything discrete and sequential, and diffusion or flow models for anything continuous.
