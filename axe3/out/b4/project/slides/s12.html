Session 8 of 10 · Deep generative models

[1 click] The idea of diffusion fits in one picture. Start from data, on the left: here, points on a ring. Add a little Gaussian noise. Add a little more. After enough steps nothing of the structure remains: pure Gaussian noise, on the right. This forward process is fixed; it has no parameters. Now train a network to undo one step: given a noisy sample and the noise level, estimate the noise that was added. To generate, draw pure noise and apply the learned denoising step repeatedly, from right to left. A hard problem, generating structure from nothing, has been cut into many easy ones: removing a little noise.
