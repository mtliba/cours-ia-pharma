Session 8 of 10 · Deep generative models

[1 click] Any joint distribution over a sequence factorizes exactly into a product of conditionals: the first element, the second given the first, and so on. This is just the chain rule of probability. An autoregressive model represents every conditional with one network, a causal transformer, that outputs a distribution over the vocabulary. Take minus the logarithm and you recover the next-token loss from session six: training is maximum likelihood, with an exact likelihood. Large language models are this. So are SMILES generators for molecules, and Delphi-2M for disease histories. To generate, sample the first token, feed it back, sample the next.
