Session 8 of 10 · Deep generative models

[1 click] Evaluation is the weak point of the field. There is no single number. For text, perplexity measures fit, not truth. For images, the Fréchet inception distance compares feature statistics; it says nothing about clinical plausibility. For molecules, the standard quartet is validity, uniqueness, novelty and diversity, then drug-likeness and synthetic accessibility. But a valid molecule may be impossible to make, and a makeable one may be inactive: only an assay decides. For synthetic patients, three axes: fidelity to real distributions, utility, meaning a model trained on synthetic data works on real data, and privacy. Note the tension: perfect fidelity can simply mean the model memorized real patients.
