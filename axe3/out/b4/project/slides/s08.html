The sampling temperature. T below 1 concentrates on the top candidates; T above 1 flattens the distribution.

Here the implausible “50 mg” has probability 0.01 % at T = 0.5, 0.5 % at T = 1 and 1.9 % at T = 1.5.

A hallucination is not a malfunction: it is a sample.

Session 8 of 10 · Deep generative models

[1 click] At generation time, the model gives a score, a logit, to every candidate token. We divide the logits by a temperature T and apply a softmax. The bars show an illustrative case: the continuation of the sentence reduce tacrolimus to. At low temperature almost all the mass is on the top candidates. At temperature one point five the implausible fifty milligrams receives about 2 percent: one answer in fifty. This is the right way to think about hallucination. The model returns a plausible continuation under its learned distribution. Nothing in the objective checks the continuation against a source. Lower temperature reduces the rate; it does not make the model verify anything.
