Session 8 of 10 · Deep generative models

[1 click] Flow matching is the formulation most recent models use. Pair a noise sample x zero with a data sample x one, and draw a straight line between them. At time t, the point on the line is x t. Its velocity along the line is constant: x one minus x zero. Train a network to predict that velocity from x t and t. Again a regression. To generate, draw noise and integrate the ordinary differential equation defined by the learned velocity field, from time zero to time one. Because the target paths are straight, a few integration steps suffice. Diffusion and flow matching are close relatives: different choices of path between noise and data.
