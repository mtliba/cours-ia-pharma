One matrix: r (d + k) = 8 × (768 + 768) = 12,288. Two matrices in each of 12 blocks: 24 × 12,288 = 294,912 weights, about 0.27 % of 110 million.

n = 19, α = 0.1: ⌈20 × 0.9⌉ = 18. q̂ is the 18th smallest score: 3.1 ng/mL.

Interval: 9.0 ± 3.1 = [5.9 ; 12.1] ng/mL.

The guarantee fails under shift: another assay, another centre, a new formulation, a paediatric population.

A wide interval is information: it says the model is not ready for that decision.
