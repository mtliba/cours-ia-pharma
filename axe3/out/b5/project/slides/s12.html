Session 9 of 10 · Foundation models and adaptation

[1 click] A language model stores knowledge in its weights, imprecisely, and frozen at its training date. For drug information that is not acceptable. Retrieval-augmented generation separates knowledge from language. The question is embedded and used to search a curated corpus: summaries of product characteristics, guidelines, the unit's own protocols. The most relevant passages are placed in the prompt, with their sources. The model answers from them and cites them. The weights are unchanged, and updating knowledge means updating the corpus. What this fixes: outdated or missing knowledge, and traceability. What it does not fix: the model can still misread a passage or ignore it. Evaluate retrieval and generation separately.
