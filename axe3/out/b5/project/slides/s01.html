Part II · Advanced track · Session 9 of 10

Foundation models and adaptation

From a large pre-trained model to a small clinical dataset: prompting, probing, low-rank adaptation, retrieval, and how to trust the result.

Marouane Tliba

Session nine. Take the transformer of session six, train it with the self-supervised objectives of sessions six to eight on a very large corpus, and you obtain what is now called a foundation model. Most of you will never pre-train one. You will adapt one. This session is about that step: which adaptation strategy fits which data budget, and how to know whether the adapted model can be trusted.
