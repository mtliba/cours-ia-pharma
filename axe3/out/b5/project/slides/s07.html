Session 9 of 10 · Foundation models and adaptation

[1 click] One example in detail, because it is close to what a pharmacology unit might build. Delphi-2M modifies the GPT architecture to model sequences of diagnoses over a lifetime. It was trained on four hundred thousand participants of the UK Biobank. It was then evaluated on one point nine million individuals from Danish registries, without changing a single parameter: a true external validation, in another country and another health system. It predicts the rates of more than a thousand diseases given a person's history, with accuracy comparable to single-disease models, and it can sample synthetic future trajectories. The authors also report biases learned from the training data. Read that paper as a template for how to validate.
