Session 9 of 10 · Foundation models and adaptation

[1 click] Here are the options, ordered by what they cost. Zero-shot prompting: describe the task in words, no labels. In-context examples: put a few solved cases in the prompt. Linear probe: freeze the model, train a linear layer on its representations. Low-rank adaptation and other adapters: train a small number of additional weights. Full fine-tuning: update everything. And at the top, in red, training from scratch, which is what we are trying to avoid. The labels under each step are orders of magnitude, not rules. The method is to start at the bottom and climb only when the step below is clearly insufficient, with a baseline measured at every step.
